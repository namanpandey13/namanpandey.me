Positioning
A public workbench for sharp questions, practical systems, and conversations that turn into receipts.

The brand should feel finished enough to trust, but never polished into distance. It is builder energy with editorial taste: plain structure, strong type, one dangerous red.

Type system
IBM Plex Sans
Primary identity face. Huge headlines, page titles, section labels. Use 600-700 weight, tight tracking, confident line height.
IBM Plex Mono
Navigation, metadata, dates, tags, tiny system language. Uppercase with generous tracking.
Libre Baskerville
New secondary font. Use for essay pull quotes, intros, reflective notes, and taste-led moments where the brand needs warmth.
Usage rules
01
Keep layouts gridded, quiet, and editorial. Let the type carry the room.
02
Use deep red as a signal, not wallpaper. One red moment per composition is enough.
03
Pair brown/brass with paper for archive energy; pair red with ink for media energy.
04
Copy should sound like: useful, restless, specific. Avoid guru-speak and soft-focus inspiration.
